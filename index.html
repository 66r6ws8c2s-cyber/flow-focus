Bullish = calls bought at ask + puts sold at bid; bearish = the reverse. Counts single-leg orders of $25K+ since 9:30 AM ET and skips spreads/multi-leg, stock-tied, mid-price and deep in-the-money prints. The % is the share of directional money that is bullish (60%+ bullish, 40% or less bearish). Vs normal = order counts vs the previous 10 trading days at this time of day. Top lines are the largest $200K+ orders (buyer = paid the ask, seller = hit the bid). Open More on a card for counts vs normal, the $200K+ split, the expiry split, the checklist, and up to five big orders.
Cards are grouped Bullish, Bearish, then Neutral: a significant call sets the group, otherwise lean 55%+ is bullish and 45% or less is bearish.
The line at the top of each card is a rule-based call, not trade advice.
